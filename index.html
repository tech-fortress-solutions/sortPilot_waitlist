How SortPilot works

Built for merchants who need their collections to reflect reality — instantly

SortPilot is built around three capabilities that give Shopify merchants genuine, verifiable control over how their collections perform.

⚡

Sub-90-second webhook sorting

SortPilot listens to Shopify's inventory webhooks directly. The moment a variant hits zero, SortPilot catches the event, evaluates your rules, and reorders the collection — before your customer can click through twice.

Webhook-driven · Event-based
🗂️

True per-collection rule independence

Each collection has its own completely independent rule set — different sort modes, signals, pins, and location assignments. Changing one collection has zero effect on any other. This is an architectural guarantee, not a UI toggle.

Architecturally guaranteed
📋

Sync Transparency Log

Every sort event is logged with a timestamp, the trigger, the position change, and the latency in milliseconds. "No change" events are logged too — so you can see the app evaluated and made a deliberate decision not to act.
