The real problem

Shopify does not know which products should be shown first for each collection.

Native sorting is fine for small catalogs. It breaks down when products sell out, new items launch, seasonal collections change, and one product belongs in multiple collections with different priorities.

🛑

Sold-out products stay visible

Customers click products they cannot buy. You usually notice only after the collection already looks messy.

🧱

Manual drag-and-drop does not scale

Once a collection has hundreds of products, manually arranging products becomes a recurring chore.

🗂️

One global rule is not enough

New Arrivals, Sale, Bestsellers, and Clearance collections need different rules — not one shared sorting logic.

Fit check

This beta is not for every Shopify store.

Good beta feedback comes from stores where collection order actually affects sales.

Good fit

  • Stores with 300+ products and several active collections.
  • Stores where products regularly sell out, restock, or rotate seasonally.
  • Merchants who currently sort manually or are unhappy with delayed sorting.
  • Shopify agencies managing product-heavy client stores.

Not a good fit yet

  • Tiny stores where collection order barely changes.
  • Dormant stores, test stores, or stores without real sales activity.
  • Stores needing a full AI personalization suite on day one.
  • Merchants who cannot share basic feedback during beta.
